# Google Play store listing

Ready to paste into Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**.
Written for **long-tail searches**: specific phrases a parent, teacher or student actually types, where a new app can reach the top results. Broad words ("reading app", "quiz", "study app") are left out on purpose until the app has ratings.

Play rules to keep: no keyword lists or repeated keywords, no "#1/best/top" claims, no other apps' names, no unattributed reviews. Google also reads the full description for search, so the phrases appear in normal sentences.

## Full description (972/4000)
```
Codebusters Speed Guide helps Science Olympiad Codebusters teams solve faster. It's free, with no ads and no accounts.

Practise with over 3,000 questions from real past tournament tests, for Division B and Division C:
• Aristocrats (K1, K2 and K3 alphabets), patristocrats and xenocrypts
• Baconian, Morse-based (fractionated Morse, Pollux) and checkerboard ciphers
• Hill, Porta, Vigenère, affine, Caesar and Atbash
• Nihilist substitution, complete columnar, rail fence and homophonic ciphers
• Cryptarithms

Turn on a timer to track your solve times, run speed drills, and learn with Morse and Baconian flashcards. Your progress and best times are saved on your device.

Practice questions come from free, public Codebusters tests published by the Toebes ciphers project. This app is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Science Olympiad.

Codebusters Speed Guide is part of LIYbrary, the Learn It Yourself library of free practice apps.
```
