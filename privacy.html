Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Codebusters Speed Guide does not collect, upload or share any personal information. It has no accounts, ads or analytics.

Your progress, best times and settings are saved only on your device (browser storage). Clearing the app's data erases them.

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which, like any website, sees your device's IP address when the font files are requested. Demo narration uses your device's built-in speech voice. Nothing else is sent anywhere, and the app works offline once it has loaded.

Practice questions come from the free, public Codebusters tests published by the Toebes ciphers project.

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
